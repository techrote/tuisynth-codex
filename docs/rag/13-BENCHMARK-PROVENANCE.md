# Benchmark/provenance
Comparability comes from equal requirements, not matching architecture. Append material contributions to `docs/PROVENANCE.md` with issue, role, tool/provider, exact model/config, contribution, human intervention, date. Roles: prepass, implementation, subagent, debug, review, release-audit. Unknown IDs are `unknown`, never guessed.

History should expose CI repair cycles, review findings, rework, interruptions/resume, human intervention, final software completeness and physical acceptance status.
